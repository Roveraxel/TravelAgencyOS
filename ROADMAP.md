# ROADMAP.md — Phasing, Constraints & Cost Assumptions

## Constraints

- **Founder time:** 10–15 hours/week
- **Initial budget:** ~€1,000
- **Rule:** Validate workflow and willingness to pay before committing to paid infrastructure or compliance builds.

## Phases

1. **Phase 0 — Local Prototype & Discovery (Current):**
   - Cost: €0/month (runs locally on Node.js 20+).
   - Deliver: Working local MVP, agency interviews, competitor validation.
2. **Phase 1 — Minimal Authenticated Pilot (Post-Discovery Gate):**
   - Estimated infra: ~€10–€30/month (EU VPS / managed DB).
   - Deliver: Login, team workspace, persistent DB, GDPR export/delete, 2–3 pilot agencies.
3. **Phase 2 — Operational Extensions (Post-Pilot Validation):**
   - Versioned PDF offers, supplier payment alerts, accountant-reviewed invoicing/e-Factura integration or export to existing Romanian accounting tools (e.g., SmartBill/Saga).
